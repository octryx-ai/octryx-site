Why the reranker matters more than the embedding model

Written from the research log on this site; no new findings. Problem, starting assumption, experiment, results, limitations, decision.

Problem

Search over the team’s knowledge has to find the right passage, also across languages. A misconfiguration had made it fall back silently to vectors that carried no meaning.

Starting assumption

We might do without a reranker: the open ones gave flat, wrong scores for German, and the good multilingual one had a non-commercial licence.

Experiment

We switched to real multilingual embeddings, then measured search with and without a reranker, with small local and large hosted embedding models.

Results

With real multilingual embeddings, German questions find English content. In our measurements the reranker was the biggest quality lever: with it, a small local embedding model matched large hosted ones, at no embedding cost. Fusing word and vector search without weights could even hurt a strong vector model.

Limitations

Measured on our own content and questions; the research log does not publish the scores. Other content and languages may behave differently.

Decision

Kept: the reranker is on, and a small local embedding model is enough.
