On this page

Audit, decisions and budgets ​

Built What was done, what was refused, and what it may cost. These pages are under Operations › Govern and Admin › Models.

Audit log ​

Operations › Audit log, for platform admins: who changed what, when and from where. Search by actor or object, filter by category and window (7, 30 or 90 days), and Export to CSV; the export is audited too.

Decisions ​

Operations › Decisions: every request a gate refused or held for approval, with its outcome, what was asked, who and where, the gate and the deciding rule. Filter by outcome, Workspace, gate and window (24 hours, 7 or 30 days). Allowed requests are counted, not listed.

Session transcripts ​

Off by default. A Workspace admin switches on Full session transcripts under Workspace settings › Models & agents › Coding tools & devices, card Session transcripts. Then the full Claude Code transcript, subagents included, is uploaded after a session, redacted for secrets on the machine. Only Workspace admins and the session's owner can read it, every read is audited, and transcripts are kept for 30 days by default (1 to 3,650).

Budgets ​

Admin › Models › Budgets: a budget per Workspace or per person, per day, week or month, in USD.

  • Warn at (80 % by default): an alert, listed as an attention item under Admin › Problems.
  • Stop at (100 % by default, up to 1,000 %): further requests are refused with the budget's name.
  • Budgets are switched off, never deleted.

A single work item can also have a cost budget: its run pauses when it reaches it.

Usage & costs ​

Operations › Usage & costs: spend, priced coverage, tokens and active users, by model, by Workspace (with its budget) and the top users. Costs are the platform's estimate from list prices, not an invoice; a model without a price is counted as unpriced. Seat use is shown separately from API cost.

See also Operations.
